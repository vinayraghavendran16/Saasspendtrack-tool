# Unified product roadmap — 2026-11-27
This is the canonical planning roadmap; supersedes overlapping scope descriptions in technology-aop-planner.md, reporting-optimization-roadmap.md, procurement-usage-architecture.md and vendor-governance-build.md, without deleting their implementation specifications.
## Three product bets
1. Technology spend digital twin: monthly department hiring/exits/transfers and revenue scenarios joined to effective-dated contract/SKU commitments; distinguish cash, accrual, run rate and avoidable spend; manual CSV first.
2. Commercial decision engine: compare renewal, resizing, renegotiation, reseller and consolidation alternatives on compatible SKU rights, dates, currency, minimums, exit/migration/overlap costs; no invented benchmarks; mutually exclusive savings alternatives.
3. Business outcome and savings accountability: versioned investment objectives, evidence and owner/backup, renewal decisions, actual invoice reconciliation; separate identified, approved, contracted, realized and avoided spend; no implied causal revenue attribution.

## Delivery order
A: deterministic monthly headcount and commitment projections, scenario comparisons, pure tests; B: quote/alternative economics and nonoverlap rules plus tests; C: outcome definitions and realized savings reconciliation with provenance and tests; D: additive tenant schema, authorization, optimistic versioning and audit; E: UI, CSV/XLSX, synthetic end-to-end and customer validation. Keep new modules default-off and separate from core release gates; no production merge/migration/deploy implied. Preserve existing customer data.
## Acceptance
Explain every forecast by contract, period, assumption and source; show unknown data, no implicit FX, no savings before contractual eligibility, no duplicate alternatives, no automatic cancellations or deprovisioning. Confirm role and tenant isolation, historical baseline immutability, audit trail, recovery, accessibility. Validate three design partners before claiming competitive advantage.
