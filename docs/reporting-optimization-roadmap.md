Qualitative themes: integration reliability, reporting flexibility, data accuracy, reconciliation, onboarding and workflow maintenance. Not exhaustive or frequency ranked.
P0: role-specific Finance, Procurement, IT, RevOps views backed by one metric dictionary; fiscal/calendar/custom periods, monthly/quarterly/yearly buckets, previous-period and YoY comparison; currency segregated unless explicit dated FX basis; actual invoices distinct from commitments and forecast. Export CSV and native XLSX with summary, detail, methodology, filters, as-of and source freshness; numeric cells typed, untrusted text never formulas, role authorization on export, tenant scope, no employee PII by default. Historical reports require dated observations and immutable invoice/event records, not current snapshot extrapolation.
Optimization: publisher, reseller, supplier, SKU and contract grain; equivalent pricing comparison only with comparable term, region, support, commitments and currency. Savings lifecycle identified/approved/contracted/realized, evidence and mutually exclusive alternatives, no double counting. No automatic cancellation. Customer-specific prices not represented as market benchmarks. Provider usage metrics distinguish login, active sessions, feature usage and unknown. Self-serve import preview and reconciliation, source health and freshness, role-specific onboarding.
Keep unfinished features server-disabled or excluded from release; validation required.
