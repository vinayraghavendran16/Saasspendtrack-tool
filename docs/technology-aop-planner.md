# Technology AOP and budget planner
Scope: technology expenditure planning, not general ledger or full FP&A replacement. Include in existing package subject to validated release; no pricing change. Ship behind default-off feature flag, not a prerequisite for existing core release.
## Inputs
Tenant-scoped department aggregates, opening headcount, monthly hires/exits/transfers, actual vs projected provenance, revenue targets and rolling forecast distinguished from ARR/bookings, fiscal calendar, contract/SKU commitments, unit prices, minimum quantities, renewal/notice dates, utilization, budget ceilings, approved FX assumptions. Manual/CSV first; CRM/HRMS read-only adapters later. Unknown inputs explicitly flagged; no employee names or compensation needed.
## Engine
Monthly headcount roll-forward, paired transfers, reject negative headcount. Seat demand based on department eligibility and coverage assumptions, shared pools deduplicated, minimum commitments until contractual reduction date, fixed and variable consumption pricing separate. Separate cash payment schedule from accrued expense and annualized run rate. Integer minor-unit amounts, explicit currencies and dated FX rates; never silently sum currencies. Revenue ratios only for compatible period/currency/metric and nonzero revenue. Base/upside/downside/custom scenarios, sensitivity and forecast vs actual. Savings identified vs approved vs realized distinct; no double counting. Do not treat projected exits as authorization to revoke access.
## Workflow
Draft -> submitted -> approved/locked; revisions and audit, department budget owner scoped access; CFO approval, CRO GTM views, procurement contract actions, IT seat demand. Role grants explicitly scoped, no implicit authority from job title. Immutable baseline, rolling forecasts, variance bridge, Excel export with formula injection protections, assumptions/source freshness and missing-data warnings. No automatic purchases or cancellations.
## Delivery
P1 pure deterministic monthly engine and tests. P2 additive tenant-scoped schema and APIs behind flag with authorization and concurrency guards. P3 UI, CSV/XLSX, manual import, approval workflow. P4 read-only integrations, live synthetic preview and security/recovery tests. Existing release remains separate; no production migration/deploy authorization inferred.
