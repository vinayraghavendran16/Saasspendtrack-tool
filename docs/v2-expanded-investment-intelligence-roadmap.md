# Expanded technology investment intelligence roadmap
Approved direction 2026-11-27. V1 remains frozen; no production deployment or paid procurement authorized by this roadmap.
## Delivery sequence
1. Finish evidence-linked renewal decision lifecycle: register, detail, evidence access audit, challenge, reassessment, approval, financial verification; test role, tenant, stale revision, demo isolation and live staging.
2. Discovery and integrations: read-only Google Workspace, Microsoft Graph, accounting imports first; OAuth least privilege, encrypted credentials, revocation, incremental sync, provenance, deduplication, reconciliation, quotas. No automatic destructive actions.
3. Procurement and negotiation: intake, existing alternatives, approved budget, quote comparisons, contract terms, human-approved negotiation drafts.
4. Dependency graph and decision simulation: evidence-backed relationships, uncertain links, impact scenarios, migration cost, no invented dependencies.
5. Financial and adoption outcomes: consumption units, AI spend, invoice anomalies, approved baselines, attribution and population integrity.
6. Action Centre and execution: approval-bound idempotent jobs, preflight, retry, audit and recovery; explicit authorization before external actions.
7. Tenant learning and executive intelligence: reviewed outcomes only, transparent metrics, no unsupported causal claims.
8. Market intelligence: tenant consent, licensing review, aggregation thresholds, privacy review, no cross-tenant raw data; defer paid benchmark licensing until validated demand.
## Ten engines
Financial, Decision, Action Centre, Dependency Graph, Tenant Learning, Executive, Discovery/Integration, Procurement/Negotiation, Adoption/Consumption, Market/Benchmark. Engines are not mandatory additional navigation modules.
## Gates
Default off; design, schema, API, UI, tests, security, performance, documentation, accessibility and staging evidence before activation. Do not claim production readiness from synthetic tests. Maintain one authoritative source per financial metric. Separate projected, prevented, realized and attributed savings. Do not spend money, enable paid services or migrate production without owner approval.
## Cost assumptions
Founder-led AI-assisted engineering: zero developer cash cost. Target incremental build and beta cash budget INR 1–2 lakh, excluding existing subscriptions, independent penetration testing, formal compliance and commercial datasets. This is a planning target, not a spending authorization or guaranteed ceiling. Founder time and independent technical review remain necessary. Prefer existing tools, direct APIs and limited read-only pilots. Track actual AI tokens, connector maintenance, storage, monitoring and customer-level cost before pricing changes. Review monthly unit economics at 10, 25 and 100 customers.
