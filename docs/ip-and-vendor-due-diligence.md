# IP and vendor due diligence
Status: evidence checklist, not legal opinion or verified compliance.

## IP ownership
Collect incorporation and legal name evidence for Whizzlead Solutions OPC; verify exact registered name before filing. Inventory repositories, domains, trademarks, source code, design assets, documentation and contractor contributions. Obtain written assignments where required. Record third-party and AI-assisted code provenance, licence obligations, notices and exceptions. Generate a dependency inventory and SBOM from the pinned release lockfile; review transitive dependencies and copyleft obligations. Keep secrets and customer data out of repositories.

## Trademark
Search exact and similar marks in India and US official registers and relevant common-law sources. Evaluate descriptiveness of SaaS Spend Track, confusion risk, word versus device mark, and classes 9 and 42 with counsel. Record search date, scope and results. No clearance, filing or registration asserted.

## Vendor evidence
Cloudflare: DPA, subprocessors, data locations, access controls, backup and incident terms.
Anthropic: applicable API terms, retention/training settings, DPA and transfer safeguards.
ZeptoMail: transactional email data, retention, DPA and processing regions.
Razorpay: payment processing roles, contractual terms, retention and security assurance.
For each vendor record purpose, data categories, controller/processor role, regions, transfer mechanism, assurance expiry, owner and review date. Verify actual account settings rather than assuming vendor marketing applies.
RB2B: assess marketing-site tracking separately, lawful basis, transparency, consent requirements and authenticated app exclusion.

## Release evidence
No licence scan, trademark search, signed assignment or executed vendor agreement is verified by this checklist. Obtain evidence before making external compliance claims.
