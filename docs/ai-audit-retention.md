# AI audit retention
Proposed policy: retain payload-free egress metadata for 365 days. Consent and lock history are separate security records and are not covered by this deletion policy. Do not enable automated deletion until customer disclosure, export, backup policy and release tests are approved. Audit export is workspace-scoped, paginated and contains no prompts or responses. Authorization records do not establish delivery; missing outcome records are indeterminate. Audit history begins at deployment, not account creation. Logs are not independently tamper-evident. Do not infer zero transmission from missing records alone.
