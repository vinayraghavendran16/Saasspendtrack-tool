      Alternative: non-PII datasheet

      Prefer not to share employee names or emails? Use the template below. It carries only the fields the rollup needs: opaque employee code, department, role band, cost centre, region.

      1. Download the template

      Two shapes supported. Either records one anonymised row per person, or an aggregated row per department. Both skip names, emails, phone numbers and other PII.

      2. Upload or link the datasheet

      Upload the completed template as .csv, .tsv or .xlsx; or paste a Google Drive / Google Sheets / OneDrive link. We'll read it, validate the headers, and refuse to import anything that looks like PII.

      Drop the completed template here or

      Privacy stance. The non-PII datasheet is designed to preserve the department rollup and seat-waste signals without carrying individual identifiers. When the file is parsed, any column that looks like a name, email, phone number, address, or government ID is rejected with an inline error. The tool keeps only the fields the rollup needs.

      Alerts

      90 / 60 / 30-day renewal warnings with owner acknowledgement and manager escalation.

      Every active subscription fires a warning at 90, 60, and 30 days before renewal. If the owner doesn't acknowledge within 3 days of the trigger, it escalates to their manager. This app can't send from your browser, but it prepares the exact email and Slack payload for each alert so renewals stop depending on one person remembering.
